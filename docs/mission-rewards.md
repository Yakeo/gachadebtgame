# Progression mission rewards

All missions are one-time per save and use the existing, server-authoritative
claim action. Existing guest and registered saves qualify from their saved
progress immediately; no replay, new account, or currency reset is required.
Previously published mission IDs and rewards are unchanged.

The 24 additional milestones provide **24,480 gems (153 pulls)** and
**960,000 credits** in total, earned over the following progression:

| Track | New milestone goals | Added gems | Added credits |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Different campaign stages cleared | 3, 8, 10, 13, 15, 18, 20, 23 | 6,240 | 350,000 |
| Recruitment pulls, including duplicates | 1, 25, 50, 100, 200, 300 | 8,640 | 225,000 |
| Different characters owned | 4, 10, 15, 20, 30 | 4,800 | 160,000 |
| Highest owned character level | 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 | 4,800 | 225,000 |

Every new mission awards both currencies. The collection's fourth-character
milestone can be reached through the existing free UR selector. Training grants
help fund further levels; the campaign grants are available between chapters.
For scale, raising one character from level 1 to 50 costs 245,000 credits under
the current level-pricing curve. The added credits cover almost four such
upgrades, distributed rather than granted at account creation.

These are milestones, not repeatable farms. Pull rewards do not advance pull
counts or pity, and a claimed mission cannot be redeemed again after reloading,
logging in, or linking the same guest save to an account. Daily council gifts
and replay-stage credits remain the recurring sources; their rewards, starting
balances, and the welcome-gift mission are unchanged by this update.
